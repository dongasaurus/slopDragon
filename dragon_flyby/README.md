# Dragon Flyby

An animated dragon swoops across your dashboard every few minutes. Holiday themes bring Santa's sleigh, witches, skeletons and the Easter bunny.

- Flies every 15 minutes for 5 seconds by default (up to 10 seconds if you like).
- Flies over the dashboard without changing anything underneath. Taps go straight through it.
- Four original dragons inspired by *How to Train Your Dragon*.
- Themes for Christmas (snowy or Aussie), New Year, Lunar New Year, Valentine's, St Patrick's, Easter, Halloween, Día de los Muertos, Diwali, Eid, birthdays, the weather and Minecraft, or `auto` to follow the calendar.

See the **Documentation** tab for setup and options.
